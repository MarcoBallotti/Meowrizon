# Regions and sites

Reviewed against `backend/catalog/` on **2026-11-15**. Catalog definitions are versioned project inputs, not a live mission-status service. The [scientific criteria](criteri-algoritmo.md) define interpretation; the [data guide](guida-dati.md) describes terrain sources.

## Region, point and grid are different objects

The catalog retains **13 historical NASA 2022 Artemis candidate polygons**, distributed through Moon Trek. Two regions are active for the CLI V1 demonstration: **de Gerlache Rim 2** and **Malapert Massif**. The web catalog can compare all available polygon centers and sites without using that development flag as an exclusion.

The region sector is south of **84°S**; intersecting polygons are retained whole. Sites may be anywhere on the Moon. A future 100 × 100 m regional sampling cell, the **100 m diameter circular slope support**, and a landing dispersion footprint are different concepts. A point/center result must not be presented as complete regional coverage or safety.

## Polygon provenance

```text
https://trek.nasa.gov/moon/trekarcgis2/rest/services/A3_Named_regions_SP/MapServer/0/query?where=1%3D1&outFields=label&returnGeometry=true&f=json
```

The native response contains label and polygon vertices in metres in lunar south-polar stereographic coordinates, on a **1,737,400 m sphere**. Use `f=json` and transform with the verified lunar CRS; the service rejects lunar GeoJSON output. Native polygons are squares with corners on 100 m multiples. The source typo “Peak Near Shakleton” is retained separately from the corrected display name.

Compute centers in projected metres before transforming to latitude/longitude. Averaging vertex longitudes near the pole previously displaced a center by almost 8° longitude. Areas use the lunar sphere, since stereographic projection does not preserve area.

The 2024 NASA down-selection is historical context, not a reason to invent absent polygons. In particular, the Leibnitz Beta Plateau / Mons Mouton name association does not prove identical geometry. Membership flags below reproduce the earlier local catalog and literal name matches, not a fresh reconciliation with all later mission plans.

`python -m app.cli init-regions` validates source presence, coordinates and dates, acquires polygons and generates GeoJSON. For sector sites it also derives projected location and catalog associations. Catalog metadata and original uncertainty must accompany assessments.

Luna 9's historical position is uncertain by kilometres and cannot validate the actual lander's 100 m terrain/horizon. Planned targets may use a formation or candidate-region center rather than a published touchdown coordinate. Future-mission names are catalog labels requiring separate status verification before public schedule claims. Geometric Earth invisibility on the far side does not imply a relay mission is impossible.

HTTP terrain now supports nonpolar sites through a coarse global DEM; adequate 100 m slope support is not inferred from that grid. Missing measurements remain unknown with reasons. A single point cannot establish regional distributions, connected areas, persistent zones or permanent shadow.
